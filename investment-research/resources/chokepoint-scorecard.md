# Chokepoint Scorecard

Use this scorecard when running `Chain Trace` stage.

A chokepoint is not just a popular theme.

A real chokepoint is a narrow physical or institutional constraint that downstream demand cannot easily bypass.

## Six factors

Score each factor from 0 to 5. Use evidence, not vibe.

| Factor | Question | Evidence examples | Score |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Demand rigidity | 下游增长是否必然需要它？ | customer capex, orders, bill of materials, attach rate | 0–5 |
| Supply concentration | 能稳定供应的公司是否很少？ | market share, qualified supplier list, customer qualification | 0–5 |
| Expansion difficulty | 扩产是否慢、贵、难认证？ | capex cycle, yield ramp, equipment lead time, environmental/policy limits | 0–5 |
| Substitution difficulty | 替代方案是否不成熟或代价高？ | performance tradeoff, standards, customer redesign cost | 0–5 |
| Customer switching cost | 客户换供应商是否困难？ | qualification cycle, software/tooling lock-in, safety/reliability certification | 0–5 |
| Economic capture | 供应窄是否能转化为收入、毛利率或现金流？ | pricing, gross margin, backlog, contract terms, revenue purity | 0–5 |

Total score: 30.

## Interpretation

| Score | Meaning | Report treatment |
|---:|---|---|
| 25–30 | Strong chokepoint candidate | Can be top research priority if evidence is strong. |
| 19–24 | Potential chokepoint | Discuss as candidate; show missing evidence. |
| 13–18 | Beneficiary, not necessarily chokepoint | Do not call it a hard bottleneck. |
| 0–12 | Mostly theme exposure | Put in concept-only or low-priority bucket. |

## Public-company mapping rules

A real chokepoint does not automatically become a good public-company research idea.

Before elevating a company, check:

1. **Revenue purity**: how much revenue actually comes from the chokepoint.
2. **Economic capture**: whether scarcity can raise price, margin, backlog, or cash flow.
3. **Financial baseline**: revenue, margin, cash flow, balance sheet, valuation, liquidity.
4. **Customer concentration**: whether one or two customers control the economics.
5. **Capex burden**: whether growth requires heavy investment before returns appear.
6. **Valuation pull-forward**: whether the market already prices the chokepoint story.

If these are missing, the company can be listed as `needs research`, not as a candidate recommendation.

## Common mistakes

- Confusing revenue exposure with chokepoint control.
- Treating “AI-related” as “AI bottleneck”.
- Treating supply tightness as automatic profit capture.
- Ignoring customer qualification cycles.
- Ignoring capex cycles and capacity additions.
- Ignoring price competition after expansion.
- Ignoring customer concentration.
- Ignoring valuation already pricing in the thesis.

## Final wording rules

Use:

- “研究优先级高”
- “潜在卡点”
- “证据支持供应偏窄，但经济捕获待验证”
- “概念相关，尚不能证明核心瓶颈”

Do not use:

- “必然受益”
- “确定性卡点” without evidence
- 直接交易结论
- 目标价语言
- 仓位语言

# Bear Case Checklist

Use this checklist when running `Thesis Audit` stage.

## Core question

If this thesis is wrong, where is it most likely wrong?

## Checklist

### 1. Technical substitution

- Is there a cheaper or better route?
- Is the assumed architecture stable?
- Could the downstream customer bypass this layer?

### 2. Supply expansion

- Are competitors adding capacity?
- Is the bottleneck temporary?
- Will new capacity destroy pricing power?

### 3. Customer internalization

- Could the customer make it in-house?
- Could the customer dual-source quickly?
- Does the company depend on one customer?

### 4. Pricing pressure

- Is the market becoming commoditized?
- Are gross margins already peaking?
- Are Chinese or low-cost suppliers entering?

### 5. Valuation pull-forward

- Has the market already priced in 2–3 years of growth?
- Does the thesis still work at current valuation?
- What revenue and margin are required to justify the price?

### 6. Liquidity and reflexivity

- Is this a small-cap stock with thin liquidity?
- Is average daily value traded too low for the thesis audience?
- Are bid/ask spreads, options spreads, or market depth a meaningful cost?
- Are lock-up expiry, dilution, margin financing, or forced-selling risks relevant?
- Did it move because of social-media attention?
- Could the same attention reverse quickly?

### 7. Correlation and sympathy risk

- Is the stock mostly moving with an index, sector ETF, or theme basket?
- Is it a sympathy play rather than a company-specific chokepoint?
- Does correlation rise during selloffs?
- Would the thesis fail if the hot basket de-rates?
- Are peers with similar exposure cheaper, cleaner, or less risky?

### 8. Estimate revision risk

- Have EPS or revenue estimates already been revised upward?
- Is the estimate range wide, suggesting uncertainty?
- Are revisions broad-based or driven by a few analysts?
- What would trigger a downward revision?
- Does the thesis rely on management guidance that may be conservative or promotional?

### 9. Evidence quality

- Which claims are facts?
- Which claims are assumptions?
- Which claims are rumors?
- What is the weakest link in the chain?

## Kill criteria

Write 3–7 concrete conditions that would weaken or kill the thesis.

Examples:

- Key customer not confirmed.
- Product qualification delayed.
- Competing technology wins.
- Capacity additions reduce pricing.
- Downstream capex slows.
- Gross margin fails to improve.
- Company dilutes shareholders heavily.
- Estimate revisions turn negative.
- Liquidity dries up or lock-up expiry creates selling pressure.
- Theme-basket correlation breaks the company-specific thesis.

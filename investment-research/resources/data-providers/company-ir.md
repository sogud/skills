# Company IR Playbook

## Best for

Company primary materials outside formal filing databases: investor presentations, earnings calls, product pages, press releases, investor day decks, webcasts, and official company explanations.

## Setup Mode

- Built-in or Manual fallback for public IR pages.
- Web login or Browser login session for investor event archives requiring sign-in.
- Paid terminal / subscription for transcript databases.

## Source Tier

Tier 2: Company primary source.

## What it can support

- Company-stated strategy, product positioning, segment framing, and management commentary.
- Management explanation of demand, supply, customers, capex, and risks.
- Product existence and official technical descriptions.

## What it cannot support

- Independent validation of management claims.
- Audited financials unless the IR page hosts official filings.
- Customer revenue unless specifically disclosed.
- Market-wide supply/demand without external confirmation.

## Query / navigation patterns

- Company name + investor relations.
- Latest presentation + latest earnings call + latest annual report.
- For product claims, use official product pages and technical brochures.
- For trend changes, compare current presentation with older presentations.

## Required provenance

Preserve title, company, publication/event date, URL, slide/page number or transcript speaker/section, and exact claim.

## Fallbacks

- Exchange filings.
- SEC/HKEX/CNINFO hosted versions.
- Transcript provider if company transcript is unavailable.

# Google Patents / Lens Playbook

## Best for

Patent and standards-adjacent research: technical routes, assignee activity, manufacturing processes, material formulas, possible moats, and technology substitution paths.

## Setup Mode

- Manual fallback for Google Patents.
- Web login or Manual fallback for The Lens.
- Manual fallback for official patent offices when needed.

## Source Tier

Tier 3 for patent databases; Tier 1 when using official patent office records.

## What it can support

- Evidence of R&D activity.
- Technical route and process details.
- Assignee/inventor patterns.
- Prior art and competing approaches.
- Possible technical moat hypotheses.

## What it cannot support

- Product commercialization.
- Revenue or customer traction.
- Patent quality without legal/technical analysis.
- A company's current production capacity.

## Query / navigation patterns

- Search by company assignee, inventor, core technical terms, CPC/IPC classification, and date range.
- Check patent family, publication date, assignee, claims, and legal status when available.
- Compare patents with standards, product pages, and filings before making moat claims.

## Required provenance

Record patent title, publication/application number, assignee, publication date, jurisdiction, family/legal status if available, URL, and claim/abstract excerpt.

## Fallbacks

- USPTO, WIPO Patentscope, Espacenet, CNIPA.
- Standards bodies when the thesis depends on adoption rather than patents.

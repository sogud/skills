# Grok / X Playbook

## Best for

Real-time social narrative, rumor origin, crowding, influencer amplification, and market reflexivity, especially for US/global stories that live on X.

## Setup Mode

- Web login.
- Browser login session.
- Paid subscription may be required for Grok or advanced X access.

## Source Tier

Tier 5: Social / forum / influencer narrative.

## What it can support

- A narrative exists and is spreading.
- Who is amplifying a claim.
- Approximate timing of narrative emergence.
- Crowding and sentiment clues.
- Claims that need primary-source verification.

## What it cannot support

- Company fundamentals.
- Customer relationships.
- Revenue, orders, or margins.
- Technical truth.
- Any Strong Fact without primary confirmation.

## Query / navigation patterns

- Search exact claim phrases.
- Search ticker/company plus product/customer keywords.
- Sort by latest and identify earliest visible posts when possible.
- Preserve original posts for anti-hype mode.
- Pair any claim with filings, IR, customer announcements, patents, or technical sources.

## Required provenance

Preserve author/handle, timestamp, original post URL, exact claim, engagement context if relevant, and whether it is confirmed elsewhere.

## Fallbacks

- X manual search.
- Reddit, Stocktwits, Seeking Alpha comments.
- For China/HK narratives, use 雪球、东方财富股吧、同花顺社区 and investor interaction platforms.

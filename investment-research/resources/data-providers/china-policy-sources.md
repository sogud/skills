# China Policy Sources Playbook

## Best for

Chinese official and quasi-official policy, macro, industrial, production, import/export, subsidy, procurement, and sector activity evidence.

This source layer excludes social media and financial-media narrative.

## Setup Mode

- Built-in or Manual fallback for public government pages.
- Browser automation if pages are dynamic or search forms are hard to fetch.

## Source Tier

Tier 1 for official government/regulator/statistics/customs/procurement sources.
Tier 3/4 for quasi-official industry associations depending on methodology.

## What it can support

- Policy text and effective status.
- Macro/statistical series.
- Industrial output and production data.
- Import/export evidence.
- Subsidy, tender, procurement, and project award facts.
- Sector regulator statements.

## What it cannot support

- Company revenue unless linked to company disclosures.
- Market rumors.
- Policy implementation beyond what the source states.
- Actual disbursement if only a policy intent or award is published.

- 国家统计局.
- 发改委.

- 人民银行.
- 商务部.

- 交通运输部.
- 住建部.
- 农业农村部.
- 自然资源部.

- 中国政府采购网.
- 全国公共资源交易平台.
- 中国招标投标公共服务平台.
- Local government, local statistics, local DRC/MIIT-style departments when local implementation matters.

## Query / navigation patterns

- Search policy title, issuing agency, date, draft/final/effective status, and implementation notice.
- For industry data, identify official series and data vintage.
- For procurement/subsidy, distinguish notice, bid, award, contract, and actual disbursement.
- For trade, use HS code or product category carefully.

## Required provenance

Preserve agency, document title, publication date, effective date if present, URL, policy status, table/section, and quoted Chinese excerpt.

## Fallbacks

- Official local government mirrors.
- Ministry press conferences or official interpretations.
- Quasi-official industry associations, clearly marked below official source tier.

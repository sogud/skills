# CNINFO / 巨潮资讯 Playbook

## Best for

A-share official disclosures: annual reports, semiannual reports, quarterly reports, announcements, prospectuses, shareholder changes, corporate actions, and exchange-filed materials.

## Setup Mode

- Built-in when the agent has web/fetch access.
- Manual fallback through 巨潮资讯 and exchange disclosure pages.
- Browser automation if document search requires dynamic interaction.

## Source Tier

Tier 1: Official primary source.

## What it can support

- A-share company reported financials and segment information.
- Official announcements and risk disclosures.
- Prospectus and listing documents.
- Company-stated products, customers, capacity, and projects when disclosed.

## What it cannot support

- Social narrative or stock-board claims.
- Undisclosed orders or customer relationships.
- Policy facts unless the filing cites an official policy document.

## Query / navigation patterns

- Search by stock code, company abbreviation, document type, and date.
- For company research, collect latest annual report plus latest quarterly report.
- For thesis verification, search announcement titles for products, projects, customers, capacity, fundraising, and risk warnings.
- Cross-check with 上交所、深交所、北交所 disclosure pages when needed.

## Required provenance

Preserve stock code, document title, publication date, reporting period, URL, page/table/section, and quoted Chinese excerpt.

## Fallbacks

- 上交所 / 深交所 / 北交所 official disclosure systems.
- Company IR page.
- Financial data tools only as baseline, reconciled to filings.

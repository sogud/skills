## 13. Frozen Assessment and Review

Fill before the outcome is known when making a testable judgment; factual descriptions do not need invented price forecasts.

- Exact claim, asset and direction or observable event:
- Activation condition (or unconditional):

- Success and failure criteria; evidence that invalidates the thesis:
- Next review date:

At review, keep the original claim and append the result with dated evidence. Use **not due / not triggered / cannot evaluate / supported / contradicted**. Record activation time and what could actually have been executed; a hypothetical paper result is not a real trade. Do not revise the baseline after observing the outcome.

When reporting a hit rate, state the eligible sample, numerator, denominator and exclusions; retain untriggered and failed-data cases separately. Show payoff/loss size and net benchmark-relative result when relevant. Process completion, source success, report grade and trade profitability are different metrics; no sufficient comparable sample means accuracy is unknown.
